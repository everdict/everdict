# Intent: complete the remaining evolution evidence and authority work

Author: maintainer (requested in conversation). Status: accepted
Design: none — the accepted review and evolution-review-follow-up document specify the required boundaries; the implementation plan records their concrete integration.

## Problem

Campaign allocations do not count evaluation attempts before dispatch. The adoption gate cannot distinguish non-inferiority from undetected regression. Metric strings conflate producer and criterion identities. A delegation brief does not restrict the credential used to fetch evidence.

## Proposed outcome

A durable family budget is consumed before an evaluation is dispatched, and a round can use only its bound result. Failed or unreported attempts remain spent. Versioned non-inferiority and evidence requirements produce explicit inconclusive results. New measurements carry structured identities and stable references consumed by verdict policies, while old sealed records retain their historical interpretation. Campaign delegates receive narrowly authorized target evidence without access to held-out results through sibling APIs.

## Affected users and systems

Contracts, domain, application-control/execution, database, graders, authentication, API/MCP, SDK and campaign callers.

## Constraints

Preserve historical data and policy digests. Unknown authority refuses effects. Bind authorization before effects, and test PostgreSQL races and HTTP/MCP bypasses. Do not push or deploy.
