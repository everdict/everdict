# Evolution paper companion: detailed reading notes

This companion expands the [literature review](../evolution-literature-review.md) into individual explanations of all 60 sources: WikiSkill, its 46 direct references, and 13 complementary studies. The collection contains 53 research publications and seven other resources. It covers this defined bibliography, not every publication on evolution or every recursively cited paper.

Each research note explains the problem, prerequisite principles, method, experimental conditions, results, relevant component tests, connections, limitations, and a proposed Everdict experiment. Survey and resource notes distinguish taxonomies or authoring guidance from controlled empirical evidence. English notes and Korean translations are available in the research reader; the translations render the explanations, not the full original papers. Reported experiments have not been independently reproduced.

## Reading the evidence

Versioned primary sources are cited within each note, with section or table locations for consequential claims. Percentage points are distinguished from relative improvements. Model, dataset, harness, budget, and split differences prevent a single cross-paper leaderboard. Internal inconsistencies are retained explicitly rather than silently resolved. Proposed Everdict changes are analytical recommendations, with the [first-loop decision](../evolution-first-loop.md) providing the current proposal context.
